Catdoku
  Zoodoku (for those with CVD)

Catdoku is a browser game based on the "queens" game.
No download, no install, no adds, simply open in the browser and play.
A version for those with color vision deficiency (CVD) is named Zoodoku.

Rules:
- No two cats on the same colour.
- No two cats in the same row or column.
- No two cats next to each other (including diagonal).

Play:
- Double click uncovers a cat (or an error).
- Single click marks a cell.
- The Next button generates a new puzzle at random.
- The Restart button lets you restart the current puzzle.

Enjoy!
